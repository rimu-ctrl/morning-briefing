📖 단어 (20)
  1. 01 scalable adj

    확장 가능한

    "The design system needs to be scalable across 10+ product lines."

  2. 02 seamless adj

    매끄러운, 끊김 없는

    "A seamless handoff between screens keeps users in a flow state."

  3. 03 stakeholder n

    이해관계자

    "Early stakeholder alignment prevents costly changes late in the project."

  4. 04 streamline v

    간소화·효율화하다

    "Streamlining the checkout process reduced cart abandonment by 30%."

  5. 05 succinct adj

    간결하고 명확한

    "Succinct microcopy guides users without overwhelming them."

  6. 06 synthesize v

    (정보를) 통합·종합하다

    "She synthesized findings from 20 interviews into clear design principles."

  7. 07 tangible adj

    구체적인, 실질적인

    "We need tangible outcomes from this workshop, not just sticky notes."

  8. 08 throughput n

    처리량, 처리 속도

    "Increasing server throughput reduced page load time significantly."

  9. 09 token n

    디자인 토큰 (색상·간격·폰트의 변수)

    "Design tokens ensure color consistency across web and mobile platforms."

  10. 10 tradeoff n

    트레이드오프, 상충 관계

    "There's always a tradeoff between performance and visual richness."

  11. 11 trajectory n

    궤적, 발전 방향

    "The product's trajectory shifted after the acquisition."

  12. 12 transparent adj

    투명한, 명확한

    "Being transparent about data usage builds user confidence."

  13. 13 validate v

    검증하다, 확인하다

    "Prototype testing validates assumptions before full development."

  14. 14 verbatim adv

    그대로, 원문 그대로

    "Quote user feedback verbatim in your research report for authenticity."

  15. 15 viable adj

    실행 가능한

    "We need a viable MVP that can be shipped within one sprint."

  16. 16 whitespace n

    여백 (디자인에서의 빈 공간)

    "Strategic use of whitespace improves readability and visual hierarchy."

  17. 17 abstraction n

    추상화; 핵심만 남기고 세부를 감추는 것

    "Good API design relies on abstraction to hide complexity."

  18. 18 accessibility n

    접근성

    "Designing for accessibility benefits all users, not just those with disabilities."

  19. 19 advocate v/n

    지지하다; 옹호자

    "Designers should advocate for the user in every product meeting."

  20. 20 affordance n

    행동 유도성 (디자인이 사용법을 암시하는 속성)

    "The raised button shape is an affordance that invites clicking."

💬 숙어 & 표현 (10)
  1. 21 ballpark figure idiom

    대략적인 수치·견적

    "Can you give me a ballpark figure for the dev effort required?"

  2. 22 bandwidth n (fig)

    여유 시간·여력 (비유적)

    "I don't have the bandwidth to take on another project this sprint."

  3. 23 bite off more than you can chew idiom

    감당할 수 없을 만큼 많이 맡다

    "We bit off more than we could chew by targeting three platforms at once."

  4. 24 boil the ocean idiom

    불필요하게 범위를 넓히다

    "We don't need to boil the ocean — focus on the top 3 user flows first."

  5. 25 bring to the table idiom

    기여하다, 가치를 제공하다

    "Strong visual storytelling is what she brings to the table."

  6. 26 circle back idiom

    나중에 다시 논의하다

    "Let's circle back on pricing once we have more user data."

  7. 27 deep dive idiom

    깊이 있는 분석·탐구

    "We did a deep dive into competitor apps before defining our strategy."

  8. 28 dog fooding idiom

    자사 제품을 직접 사용해 테스트하다

    "Dog fooding the app daily helps us catch bugs before users do."

  9. 29 double down idiom

    더 강하게 밀어붙이다, 배로 노력하다

    "After the positive A/B test, we doubled down on that design direction."

  10. 30 flip the script idiom

    기존 방식을 완전히 바꾸다

    "Instead of surveying, we flipped the script and had users teach us live."
